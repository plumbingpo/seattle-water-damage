Maintenance Tips for Mold Remediation

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Mold in ductwork is prevented by servicing the heating and cooling system and replacing its filters on schedule
  • Mold-resistant paint and wallboard are the better choice wherever moisture is common, such as a basement or bath
  • Address condensation on windows, pipes and cold surfaces promptly
  • Make sure crawl spaces are properly ventilated and consider installing a vapor barrier
  • Do not leave damp laundry, bath mats or spilled water sitting for long
  • Overflowing gutters send water into basements and crawl spaces, so clean them on a schedule

Signs You Need Mold Remediation in Seattle

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Headaches, allergy flare-ups or breathing symptoms that ease whenever you leave the house
  • Cleaning that never lasts, with growth returning to the same surface
  • Warping, blistered paint or wallpaper lifting off the wall, all clues to moisture you cannot see
  • Visible mold growth of any color on walls, ceilings, flooring or other surfaces
  • A slow leak under a sink, behind an appliance or in the roof that went unnoticed
  • Furniture against outside walls or items in closets developing speckled patches
  • An earthy, musty smell that will not go away, mostly in humid or closed-off spaces

Why Hire a Professional for Mold Remediation?

Removing mold is work homeowners should not attempt on their own. Without containment, scrubbing or tearing out moldy material releases spores by the millions, potentially spreading contamination through the house and making the health exposure worse than it was. Professional mold remediation uses negative air pressure containment, HEPA-filtered air scrubbers and personal protective equipment so spores are captured and contained during removal.

A professional job goes after the moisture source too, because that is why the mold grew in the first place. Removing visible mold without correcting the underlying moisture problem means the mold will return, often within weeks. Certified remediation technicians have the training and equipment to identify hidden moisture sources, dry affected areas properly and put solutions in place that prevent recurrence.
