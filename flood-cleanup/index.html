Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Day or night, 24/7, Seattle Restoration Co sends flood cleanup crews throughout Seattle to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

The first job is getting the water out quickly, and our submersible and truck-mounted pumps are able to clear thousands of gallons every hour. With the standing water gone, our IICRC-certified technicians check the contamination level and apply the right sanitizing steps, including antimicrobial treatment, HEPA air filtration and disinfection of every affected surface. We tear out and properly dispose of what cannot be saved, such as soaked carpet padding, contaminated drywall and ruined insulation.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. Our crew returns daily, using thermal imaging and calibrated meters to track moisture until the structure is completely dry and mold growth is prevented. Every stage of the job is recorded for your claim: photographs, detailed moisture maps, logs of the equipment used and the scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters require.

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • Roof runoff should leave the downspout far enough out that it cannot soak the foundation
  • Being outside a designated flood zone does not rule out flooding, so think about flood coverage
  • A battery-powered backup sump pump keeps water moving if the electricity fails in a storm
  • Keep gutters and storm drains clear of debris so water can flow properly
  • Cracks in the foundation should be sealed and the basement walls given a waterproof coating

Signs You Need Flood Cleanup in Seattle

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Rainwater getting inside through a roof or wall damaged by a storm
  • Living areas flooded by contaminated water from a backed-up drain or sewer
  • Cracked foundation walls, low doorways or window wells letting water inside
  • A sump pump that failed during heavy rain and let the basement flood
  • Standing water anywhere on your property, whatever the source
  • Soaked landscaping that leaves water pooling against your foundation
  • An overflowing washing machine, dishwasher or water heater

Why Hire a Professional for Flood Cleanup?

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. The water brings sewage, chemicals, bacteria and more with it, and all of that is a serious hazard to health. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Beyond the health concerns, a poor flood cleanup often leaves hidden moisture that turns into severe mold growth within days. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. This scientific approach to drying is the only reliable way to prevent secondary damage that can cost thousands more to fix.
