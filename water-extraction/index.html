Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. Seattle Restoration Co provides emergency water extraction throughout Seattle using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Few water removal systems match the power of a truck-mounted extractor, which draws water from structural materials, carpeting and hard flooring far better than rental or consumer machines. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

Removing visible water is only part of what professional extraction involves. Using moisture detection equipment, our IICRC-certified team finds water that has moved out of sight, whether under cabinets, beneath flooring, behind baseboards or inside walls. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

The Benefits of Professional Water Extraction

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction. Professional truck-mounted extraction equipment removes water hundreds of times faster than consumer tools, so what might take you an entire day with a shop vacuum takes our equipment minutes. That gap in speed is more than a convenience, because each hour water touches building materials can add thousands of dollars in further damage.

Our extraction also goes after moisture that home equipment can neither find nor get to. Through capillary action and wicking, water moves up drywall, into the pad under carpet, between layers of flooring and along plumbing lines and electrical conduit. Our specialty extraction tools and techniques target these hidden moisture deposits, removing significantly more water than surface extraction alone. Being this thorough shortens drying, cuts equipment costs and, above all, helps stop trapped moisture from turning into hidden mold.

Signs You Need Water Extraction in Seattle

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Wet baseboards and water creeping from beneath kitchen or bath cabinets
  • Visible water where your furnace, water heater or crawl space access is
  • Water accumulating after a fire suppression system discharges
  • Flooded window wells or stairwells letting water into the building
  • Carpeting so wet it squelches when you step on it or has puddles on top
  • Drips or a steady stream coming out of a ceiling, a wall or a fixture
  • Water sitting on a floor anywhere in the building, no matter where it came from

Maintenance Tips for Water Extraction

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Worn or aging hoses on washers, dishwashers and ice makers should be replaced before they fail
  • Place leak detection sensors in the key risk areas and test that they work
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and label it clearly
  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season so you know it will run
  • Add our number, (949) 810-8524, to your cell so help is one tap away in a water emergency
  • Regularly inspect under sinks, around toilets and near appliances for early signs of a leak
